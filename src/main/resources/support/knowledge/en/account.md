## What are the password rules?

It must have between 12 and 64 characters. We don't accept passwords that are easy to guess, such as common words, sequences like 1234 or your name or email, or ones that appeared in breaches of other sites. A long phrase is the safest.

# Two-factor authentication

## What is two-factor authentication?
keywords: what is two-factor, what is two factor, what is 2fa, what is it for 2fa, what is the authenticator

Besides your password, it asks for a 6-digit code from an app on your phone, which changes every 30 seconds. So even if someone knows your password, they can't get in. It also asks for it when adding a bank account and confirming transfers and withdrawals.

## What are recovery codes?
keywords: what are recovery codes, what is a recovery code, what are the codes for, how many codes, where are my codes, see my codes, how many codes do i have left, my recovery codes

They're 10 codes we give you when you turn on two-factor authentication. Each works once, wherever the app's code is asked for, and they're your way in if you lose your phone. **My account** shows how many you have left.

## My verification code doesn't work
keywords: wrong code, code doesn't work, code not working, invalid code, code expired, 6-digit code, six digit code, authenticator code, app code doesn't work

The code changes every 30 seconds and each one works once: wait for the next and type it in full. Also check your phone's time is set to automatic. Failed attempts count towards the 15-minute lockout.

# Identity

## How do I verify my identity?
keywords: verify identity, verify my identity, identity verification, validate my identity, my ine, upload my ine, send my ine, voter card, voter id, official id, kyc

In **My account**, under *Identity*, choose **Verify my identity**. Type your INE's details (name, CURP, voter key, CIC, citizen identifier and expiry) and upload a photo of the front, one of the back and one of your face. An analyst reviews it, usually within one business day, and we let you know when it is done.

## Why do you ask for my INE to withdraw?
keywords: asks for my ine, ask for my ine, asking for id, verify your identity, won't let me withdraw, can't withdraw to my bank, withdraw without ine, why do you need my ine

To protect your money, you can withdraw to your bank only with your identity verified. Depositing and sending money to other people don't need it. Verify it in **My account**, under *Identity*.

## My identity verification was rejected
keywords: rejected my ine, rejected my identity, rejected my verification, could not verify, couldn't verify, verification rejected, ine rejected, didn't approve my ine

In **My account**, under *Identity*, you see the reason. Fix what it says, such as a blurry photo or a mistyped detail, and send it again: you can do so up to 3 times a day. If you think it was a mistake, write "talk to a person".

## When is my INE reviewed?
keywords: review of my ine, ine review, still under review, ine under review, when is my ine approved, reviewing my ine, review time

Usually less than one business day. While it is under review you can't change your name. We let you know in the app and by email when it is done.
