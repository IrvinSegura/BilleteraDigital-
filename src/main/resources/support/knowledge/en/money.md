# Money

## What wallets do I have?
keywords: wallet, wallets, currency, currencies, dollars, pesos, mxn, usd, create wallet, open wallet, new wallet

You can have one wallet per currency: Mexican pesos (MXN) and US dollars (USD). **Wallets** shows each one, and **New wallet** opens the one you don't have yet. A wallet's currency can't be changed later.

## Where do I see my balance?
keywords: balance, my balance, see balance, hide balance, show balance, what is my balance, how much money do i have

I can't see your balance, but it shows on the main screen and in **Wallets**. **Hide balance** covers it, for example when there are people around.

## What does my balance chart show?
keywords: chart, graph, money in, money out, balance history

The main screen shows how your balance changed over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, and how much came in and went out this month.

## How do I deposit money?
keywords: deposit, deposits, top up, add money, put money, card, stripe, loading money, put money in

Go to your wallet and choose **Deposit**. Enter the amount and we take you to the secure payment page to pay by card. When you finish you come back to the app, and the money shows in your wallet as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Deposits have no fee. Each one can be up to 100,000 MXN or 5,000 USD.

## Can I deposit cash or from my bank?
keywords: cash, oxxo, store, paypal, deposit by spei, deposit from my bank, deposit from bank, payment method, payment methods, ways to pay, bank transfer

For now you can only deposit by card, from **Deposit** in your wallet.

## How do I transfer money to someone?
keywords: transfer, transfers, send money, pay someone, another user, another person, recipient email

Go to your wallet and choose **Send**. Enter the person's email, or pick them under **Recent**, the amount and, if you like, a concept of up to 140 characters. It arrives instantly.

You need a verified email to transfer, and the other person needs a wallet in the same currency.

## How do I receive money?
keywords: receive money, receive a transfer, get money, someone send me money, send me money

Share your account's email: whoever sends you money only needs that, and you need a wallet in the same currency. You can also use **Request** to share a link or a QR code. We notify you as soon as it arrives.

## Why does it ask for my password or a code when sending?
keywords: asks for my password, asks for password, asks for a code, ask for a code, confirm it's you

It's so nobody can empty your wallet with a stolen session. We ask on every withdrawal, the first time you send to someone, and on transfers of 1,000 MXN or 50 USD and up. With two-factor authentication it's your code; without it, your password.

## It can't find the person I want to send to

Check the email is spelled right. That person needs an account with that email and a wallet in the currency you're sending; if they don't have one, ask them to open it.

## What is a currency here?
keywords: what is a currency, what does currency mean, currency mean

A currency is a country's money, such as the Mexican peso or the dollar. Here each wallet keeps its balance in a single currency, MXN or USD, and doesn't change from one to the other.

# What each thing is

## What can I do with the wallet?
keywords: what can i do, what is this app for, what is the app for, what is the wallet for, what does it do, what is this app, how does the wallet work, features, options

You can open a wallet in pesos and another in dollars, deposit by card, send money to other users with their email, get paid with a link or a QR code, withdraw pesos to your bank by SPEI and download your receipts and statements.

## What is the difference between sending and withdrawing?
keywords: difference, difference between sending and withdrawing, difference between deposit and withdraw, difference between transfer and withdraw

Depositing puts money in your wallet from your card. Sending takes it to another user's wallet, instantly. Withdrawing takes it from your wallet to your bank account.

## What is SPEI?
keywords: what is spei, what does spei mean, spei mean

It's Mexico's electronic transfer system between banks. We use it to send your peso withdrawals to your bank account.

## What is a CLABE?
keywords: what is a clabe, what is the clabe, what does clabe mean, where do i find my clabe, where is my clabe

It's the 18-digit number that identifies your bank account in Mexico. You can find it in your bank's app or on your bank statement.

## What is the concept?
keywords: concept, what is the concept, what do i write in concept, description, reason, what is the concept for

It's an optional note of up to 140 characters that goes with a transfer or a request, for example "Friday's dinner". It shows in the movements and on the receipt. It can't include the signs < or >.

## What does each movement type mean?
keywords: movement type, movement types, what does it mean, movement in progress, withdrawal returned, withdrawal paid, payment refunded, transfer received, transfer sent

Deposit is money that came in by card. Transfer sent or received is money between users. Withdrawal is money that left to your bank, and Withdrawal returned means the bank rejected it and it came back to your wallet. Payment refunded is a deposit returned to your card. Adjustment credit and Adjustment debit are corrections made by our team.

## What is an adjustment credit or debit?
keywords: adjustment, adjustment credit, adjustment debit, balance adjustment, made an adjustment, what is an adjustment, balance correction

It is a correction to your balance made by our team, for example to credit a deposit that didn't show up or remove one credited twice. The movement shows the reason, and we let you know in the app and by email. If you don't agree with it, write "talk to a person".

## Can my movements be deleted?
keywords: delete movement, delete movements, remove movement, delete history, how long do you keep, are my movements deleted

No, movements aren't deleted or edited: they stay on record so your balance always matches them.
